About me

My Mission

I am here to bridge the gap between operational chaos and executive clarity. I am seeking a Management Consulting role because I thrive where business frameworks need restructuring and people need a leader who values Innovation as much as Respect. I don't just fix processes; I empower the teams that run them.

The Pivot to Chicago

In early 2025, I deliberately chose to let go of a rewarding position in a world-class company to pursue a vital family goal: supporting my wife’s career while reimagining my own in a new environment. Starting again in a global hub has been a humbling lesson. It reinforced that my true value isn’t just defined by my past achievements, but by how quickly I solve problems within unfamiliar territory. This journey isn't just about changing locations; it's about the internal work of proving my capability to a new market—and to myself.
